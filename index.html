    Md. Ismile Hossain Bhuiyan

    Veterinary & Animal Science Researcher | Advancing Animal Health & Food Safety

    I am Md Ismile Hossain Bhuiyan, with a B.Sc. in Veterinary Science and Animal Husbandry and an M.S. in Animal Science from Sher-e-Bangla Agricultural University, Bangladesh.

    My research interests connect animal science and microbiology, particularly how animal nutrition, gut health, and host–microbe interactions influence productive performance, meat quality, and disease susceptibility. I am also interested in antimicrobial resistance, foodborne pathogens, and infectious and zoonotic diseases, with a broader focus on the connections between animal health, food safety, and public health through One Health.

    My research experience spans meat quality, epidemiology, and infectious disease research, including systematic reviews and meta-analyses. I have practical experience in microbiological and molecular laboratory techniques, epidemiological investigations, and data analysis using R and SPSS. My interests also extend to microbial genomics and bioinformatics as tools for understanding microbial communities, pathogen characteristics, and their implications for animal production and health.

    My long-term goal is to pursue advanced interdisciplinary research that integrates animal science with microbiology to improve animal nutrition and production, support gut health, and enhance the quality and safety of animal-derived foods while addressing challenges in antimicrobial resistance and disease prevention.
